01 · Map prep

Roll, copy, map

Paste into the stash search to highlight maps without the selected modifiers.

"!te o|ur$|m resistances$|ills$|t reg"

This regex excludes maps with:

  • No life, mana or energy shield regeneration.
  • Less life and energy shield recovery.
  • Reduced maximum elemental resistances.
  • Reduced effect of non-curse auras.
  • Reduced block chance and less armour.

Generated and checked against regular map modifiers in poe.re (Covens, patch 3.30.0.4).

02 · Covens challenges

Your plan for 36 challenges

Start with maps you can run comfortably. The checkboxes are your notes; they are not synced with the game.

Do while mapping

Build progress every map

Atlas Additions

Finish five basic activities

Complete any five types, 30 times each. Good options while progressing: open strongboxes, activate shrines, complete ore deposits, defeat Essence monsters and defeat rogue exiles. Allocate their spawn nodes if needed.

Atlas Authorities

Finish five league activities

Counts differ. Ritual requires 50 rituals; Betrayal requires 80 Syndicate encounters. Other choices include 50 Ultimatum waves, 30 Expeditions or 30 Abysses. Multiple rituals or waves can count in one map.

Why Ritual and Betrayal? Thyworm's early Atlas plan uses Ritual to find useful spectres and Betrayal for progression and crafts, alongside map sustain. You already have a Forest Warrior, so Ritual is optional unless you enjoy it, want a better spectre, or want challenge progress. Betrayal is useful but not mandatory; run the mechanics you enjoy.

At higher tiers, continue Atlas completion, build toward Voidstones and defer expensive boss conditionals until your build is ready. You only need 36 of 40; decide which four to skip once you know your progress.

03 · Pantheon and challenge

Which maps to run

Gifted Gods: run the map and invoke at least one Fate before killing its boss. A Divine Vessel is not needed for the challenge. Since 3.30.0c map equivalents count.

  • Tukohama — Siege: Tahsin, Warmaker.
  • Abberath — Summit: Mephod, the Earth Scorcher.
  • Ralakesh — Fields: Drek, Apex Hunter.
  • Gruthkul — Cemetery: Erebix, Light's Bane.
  • Yugul — Terrace: Varhesh, Shimmering Aberration.
  • Shakari — Desert Spring: Terror of the Infinite Drifts.
  • Garukhan — Dig: Stalker of the Endless Dunes.
  • Ryslatha — the challenge guide lists Gorulis in Infested Valley, but that map rotated out in 3.30; the current replacement could not be verified. Search the Atlas for the current boss / Pantheon entry before spending a map.

For your Pantheon upgrades: the last verified choices were Lunaris and Gruthkul; today's account refresh could not confirm whether you changed them or which upgrades you have. For any missing upgrade, prioritise:

  • Lunaris — Mineral Pools: Shock and Horror; 6% reduced elemental damage taken if hit recently.
  • Lunaris — Iceberg: Yorishi, Jeinei Yuushu and Otesha; 10% chance to avoid projectiles.
  • Lunaris — Moon Temple: Sebbert, Crescent's Point; avoid projectiles that have chained.
  • Gruthkul — Cemetery: Erebix; enemies that recently hit you with attacks have 8% reduced attack speed.
  • Optional later — Abberath, Summit: Mephod; immunity to burning ground if you switch to Abberath for Exarch maps.

In 3.30, use a Divine Vessel on the map item to make it Deified, then run the correct map, kill the listed boss, and use its filled vessel. Deified bosses are tougher. If you also need its Gifted Gods entry, invoke a Fate in the same map. 3.30 Pantheon reference

04 · Atlas progression

How to earn four Voidstones

At level 76, keep completing your safe maps and following the Atlas quests; the bosses below are later goals. Socket each stone in its Atlas corner once earned.

  1. Eldritch: progress the Maven / Envoy quest until Exarch and Eater influence unlocks. Follow each quest in progressively higher maps, defeat the Black Star and Infinite Hunger when instructed, then defeat both the Searing Exarch and Eater of Worlds. Both are required for one stone.
  2. Originator: follow Eagon's Threads of the Originator quest; complete the Memory of Reverence trace and the Theatre of Lies, then talk to Eagon and defeat the Incarnation of Dread in the Moment of Reverence. Collect the quest rewards.
  3. Decayed: defeat both Shaper and Elder. Run their four guardian encounters to collect their respective fragment sets, open each boss fight and defeat both. Alternatively, defeat Uber Elder once.
  4. Ceremonial: let Maven witness map bosses, complete the invitations through Kirac and collect Crescent Splinters. Combine 10 into the Maven's Writ, open her arena and defeat the Maven.

The two paired routes grant a stone only after both bosses. Voidstone routes · Eagon quest steps

05 · Shopping priorities

Next three upgrades

Budget cap: 20c. These are the next items to search for; no suitable live listing has been verified for purchase yet.

01 · Life + resistance

Replace Mind Gyre ring

Find life, Dexterity and enough fire and cold resistance to replace the current ring's stats. Extra cold resistance gives your build a buffer against resistance penalties.

02 · Life + minions

Minion helmet

Find life, cold resistance and +1 or +2 minion gem levels. Keep four linked sockets for your Spectres, Animate Guardian, Minion Life and Meat Shield.

03 · Defence + minions

Armour / energy shield

Find a shield with life, energy shield, lightning resistance and minion damage. Keep three sockets and compare the armour gain against damage and energy shield lost.

Before buying, compare the exact item and its price in PoB. The earlier numbers were simulations that simply added life to your existing gear; they did not represent actual items for sale.

02 · Covens challenges

Your plan for 36 challenges

Start with maps you can run comfortably. The checkboxes are your notes; they are not synced with the game.

Do while mapping

Build progress every map

Atlas Additions

Finish five basic activities

Complete any five types, 30 times each. Good options while progressing: open strongboxes, activate shrines, complete ore deposits, defeat Essence monsters and defeat rogue exiles. Allocate their spawn nodes if needed.

Atlas Authorities

Finish five league activities

Counts differ. Ritual requires 50 rituals; Betrayal requires 80 Syndicate encounters. Other choices include 50 Ultimatum waves, 30 Expeditions or 30 Abysses. Multiple rituals or waves can count in one map.

Easy mapping for XP and currency

  1. Complete unfinished maps for Atlas points: make T1–5 maps magic or rare, and T6–10 maps rare. Choose manageable modifiers so you can stay alive and keep earning XP.
  2. Allocate map-drop and adjacent-map nodes first, then Shrine nodes. Use Big Ducks' 3.30 progression guide: map nodes first, followed by shrines.
  3. Clear monsters and kill the boss, taking nearby shrines as you go. Open safe strongboxes you find. Shaped Regions can add free Threads to maps; use them when their encounters are manageable.
  4. Sell valuable stackable drops through the Currency Exchange or trade. Check current prices first; profits depend on drops and market prices.
  5. Build toward higher tiers as your defences improve. Run Fates and other mechanics when their rewards or challenge progress justify the extra time and risk.

At higher tiers, continue Atlas completion, build toward Voidstones and defer expensive boss conditionals until your build is ready. You only need 36 of 40; decide which four to skip once you know your progress.

04 · Atlas progression

How to earn four Voidstones

At level 83, keep completing your safe maps and following the Atlas quests; the bosses below are later goals. Socket each stone in its Atlas corner once earned.

1. Eldritch Voidstone

  • Follow the Maven and Envoy quests until Eater and Exarch influence unlock.
  • Progress each influence quest through higher-tier maps.
  • Defeat the Infinite Hunger and Black Star when their quests ask you to.
  • Defeat both the Eater of Worlds and the Searing Exarch to earn the stone.

2. Originator Voidstone

  • Follow Eagon's Threads of the Originator quest.
  • Complete the Memory of Reverence trace and Theatre of Lies.
  • Talk to Eagon, then defeat the Incarnation of Dread in the Moment of Reverence.
  • Collect the quest rewards.

3. Decayed Voidstone

  • Defeat the four Shaper guardians and collect their fragments; open the Shaper fight and defeat him.
  • Defeat the four Elder guardians and collect their fragments; open the Elder fight and defeat him.
  • Both bosses are needed. Alternatively, defeat Uber Elder once.

4. Ceremonial Voidstone

  • Let Maven witness map bosses and complete her invitations.
  • Collect 10 Crescent Splinters and combine them into a Maven's Writ.
  • Use the Writ to open her arena and defeat the Maven.

The two paired routes grant a stone only after both bosses. Voidstone routes · Eagon quest steps

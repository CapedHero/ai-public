01 · Map prep

Roll, copy, map

Paste into the stash search to highlight maps without the selected modifiers.

"!te o|ur$|m resistances$|ills$|t reg"

This regex excludes maps with:

  • No life, mana or energy shield regeneration.
  • Less life and energy shield recovery.
  • Reduced maximum elemental resistances.
  • Reduced effect of non-curse auras.
  • Reduced block chance and less armour.

Generated and checked against regular map modifiers in poe.re (Covens, patch 3.30.0.4).

03 · Shopping priorities

Next three upgrades

Current budget cap: 20 Chaos Orbs (20c). These are PoB-tested targets, not verified purchasable listings. Test the actual item before buying; losing current stats can make a replacement worse.

01 · Life + resistance

Replace Mind Gyre

Aim for ~100 life and enough fire and cold resistance to replace the ring's 33% fire and 26% cold, plus 12 Dexterity. More cold or chaos resistance is useful.

PoB illustration: preserving every current modifier and adding 100 flat life raises life 3,085 → 3,284, and physical max hit 3,932 → 4,181. This is a hypothetical, not a craft or a real listing.

02 · Life + minions

Minion helmet

Look for +1 or +2 minion gems, ~70 life, at least 12% cold resistance, and four linked sockets compatible with Spectre, AG, Minion Life and Meat Shield.

PoB illustration: retaining the current helmet and adding 70 flat life gives 3,224 life and 4,106 physical max hit. A real replacement can differ.

03 · Defence + minions

Armour / ES shield

Target life, energy shield, armour, lightning resistance, and minion damage; preserve three sockets. The current shield provides 33% lightning resistance and 42% total minion damage.

PoB illustration: preserving the existing shield and adding 70 life gives 3,224 life and 4,106 physical max hit. A tested high-block shield lost 161 ES and the minion damage: don't buy it without weighing that cost.

All three illustrations use the same level-72 non-boss mapping configuration, Rumi's inactive, Lunaris and Gruthkul, and Purity assumed active. They isolate life only; they do not prove affix legality, availability, prices, sockets or combined gains. Recheck trade, requirements and PoB before purchasing.

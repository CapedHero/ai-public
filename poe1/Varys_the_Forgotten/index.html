01 · Map prep

Roll, copy, map

Paste into the stash search to highlight maps without the selected modifiers.

"!te o|ur$|m resistances$|ills$|t reg"

This regex excludes maps with:

  • No life, mana or energy shield regeneration.
  • Less life and energy shield recovery.
  • Reduced maximum elemental resistances.
  • Reduced effect of non-curse auras.
  • Reduced block chance and less armour.

02 · Covens challenges

Your plan for 36 challenges

Start with maps you can run comfortably. The checkboxes are your notes; they are not synced with the game.

Do while mapping

Build progress every map

Atlas Additions

Finish five basic activities

Complete any five types, 30 times each. Good options while progressing: open strongboxes, activate shrines, complete ore deposits, defeat Essence monsters and defeat rogue exiles. Allocate their spawn nodes if needed.

Atlas Authorities

Finish five league activities

Counts differ. Ritual requires 50 rituals; Betrayal requires 80 Syndicate encounters. Other choices include 50 Ultimatum waves, 30 Expeditions or 30 Abysses. Multiple rituals or waves can count in one map.

Easy mapping for XP and currency

  1. Complete unfinished maps for Atlas points: make T1–5 maps magic or rare, and T6–10 maps rare. Choose manageable modifiers so you can stay alive and keep earning XP.
  2. Allocate map-drop and adjacent-map nodes first, then Shrine nodes. Use Big Ducks' 3.30 progression guide: map nodes first, followed by shrines.
  3. Clear monsters and kill the boss, taking nearby shrines as you go. Open safe strongboxes you find. Shaped Regions can add free Threads to maps; use them when their encounters are manageable.
  4. Sell valuable stackable drops through the Currency Exchange or trade. Check current prices first; profits depend on drops and market prices.
  5. Build toward higher tiers as your defences improve. Run Fates and other mechanics when their rewards or challenge progress justify the extra time and risk.

Ritual and Betrayal are not required for this trade-league plan. You already have your Forest Warrior, so there is no need to farm Ritual to find one.

At higher tiers, continue Atlas completion, build toward Voidstones and defer expensive boss conditionals until your build is ready. You only need 36 of 40; decide which four to skip once you know your progress.

04 · Atlas progression

How to earn four Voidstones

At level 76, keep completing your safe maps and following the Atlas quests; the bosses below are later goals. Socket each stone in its Atlas corner once earned.

1. Eldritch Voidstone

  • Follow the Maven and Envoy quests until Eater and Exarch influence unlock.
  • Progress each influence quest through higher-tier maps.
  • Defeat the Infinite Hunger and Black Star when their quests ask you to.
  • Defeat both the Eater of Worlds and the Searing Exarch to earn the stone.

2. Originator Voidstone

  • Follow Eagon's Threads of the Originator quest.
  • Complete the Memory of Reverence trace and Theatre of Lies.
  • Talk to Eagon, then defeat the Incarnation of Dread in the Moment of Reverence.
  • Collect the quest rewards.

3. Decayed Voidstone

  • Defeat the four Shaper guardians and collect their fragments; open the Shaper fight and defeat him.
  • Defeat the four Elder guardians and collect their fragments; open the Elder fight and defeat him.
  • Both bosses are needed. Alternatively, defeat Uber Elder once.

4. Ceremonial Voidstone

  • Let Maven witness map bosses and complete her invitations.
  • Collect 10 Crescent Splinters and combine them into a Maven's Writ.
  • Use the Writ to open her arena and defeat the Maven.

The two paired routes grant a stone only after both bosses. Voidstone routes · Eagon quest steps

05 · Shopping priorities

Next three upgrades

Budget cap: 20c. These are the next items to search for; no suitable live listing has been verified for purchase yet.

01 · Life + resistance

Life and chaos resistance amulet

Replace Soul Torc with at least comparable life and strength plus chaos resistance; check that all gems and gear remain usable. Aim for 30% or more chaos resistance on the item, while preserving elemental resistance caps. Check price and actual stats before buying.

02 · Life + minions

Minion helmet

Find life, cold resistance and +1 or +2 minion gem levels. Keep four linked sockets for your Spectres, Animate Guardian, Minion Life and Meat Shield.

03 · Defence + minions

Armour / energy shield

Find a shield with life, energy shield, lightning resistance and minion damage. Keep three sockets and compare the armour gain against damage and energy shield lost.

Why chaos resistance? The latest PoB check had 12%. In a controlled test, adding 30 percentage points raised the calculated chaos maximum hit from 4,415 to 6,698 (about 52%). This tests the resistance alone, not a specific available item. Aim to improve it alongside life and capped elemental resistances.

Next Spectre

Perfect Guardian Turtle is the defensive target: it provides Determination and physical damage reduction. With only two Spectre slots, replacing Carnage Chieftain trades frenzy-charge damage for defence; keep Forest Warrior for Onslaught. Check the corpse price before buying; a third slot avoids this trade-off. Thyworm's Spectre list.

Animate Guardian: next three items

  1. Legacy of Fury boots: scorch nearby enemies to support your fire damage. Check the 20c cap and the AG gem's level requirement.
  2. Cospri's Will body armour: an additional curse for the Guardian, setting up the gloves below. This replaces Belly of the Beast, so weigh the Guardian's survivability and check the price.
  3. Asenath's Gentle Touch gloves: Temporal Chains and corpse explosions for clear. Buy after Cospri's Will or another source of additional curses, so the Guardian's curse does not overwrite your Flammability.

Cheaper curse-capacity alternative: Windscream boots instead of Legacy of Fury and Cospri's Will, if you choose to prioritise the gloves. The two boots cannot be equipped together.

These are guide-based targets, not verified listings or PoB-ranked damage gains. The Guardian's current boots and gloves have not been verified. Item prices must fit the 20c cap.

Before buying, compare the exact item and its price in PoB. The earlier numbers were simulations that simply added life to your existing gear; they did not represent actual items for sale.
